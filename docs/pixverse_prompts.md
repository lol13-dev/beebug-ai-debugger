# Beebug - 45-Second Promotional Video (PixVerse Prompts)

**CRITICAL RULE FOR AI VIDEO GENERATION:** 
DO NOT ask PixVerse to generate text inside the video (like "paste Eyler..."). AI video generators are terrible at spelling and typography. 
**Generate the video completely clean (no text), and add the text overlays later using CapCut, Premiere, or Canva using a clean, modern font (like Inter or Helvetica).**

*Total Target Duration: ~45 Seconds (approx. 9 scenes, 5 seconds each).*
*Style Guidelines: Cinematic, modern, tech-focused, glossy Frutiger Aero aesthetic, dynamic lighting. NO TEXT IN VIDEO.*
